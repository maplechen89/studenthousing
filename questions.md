# Topic

Investor appetite for student housing real estate: who is buying (and
selling) purpose-built student housing, how much capital they are putting in,
and what returns they are accepting.

# Questions

1. Which types of investors are buying student housing (private equity,
   institutional funds, REITs, pension funds, sovereign wealth funds,
   private buyers, cross-border/foreign capital), and what share of
   transaction volume does each account for?
2. Who are the largest named investors or buyers in student housing, and how
   much have they acquired or committed (dollar amount, number of beds or
   properties)?
3. How much total capital was invested in student housing transactions in
   the most recent year, and how does that compare with prior years?
4. What returns are investors accepting: what are current cap rates for
   student housing, and how do they compare with conventional multifamily?
5. What is driving or limiting investor interest, as measured by occupancy,
   pre-leasing rates, rent growth, and enrollment trends?
6. Who is selling student housing, and what share of sales come from
   developers, REITs, private owners, or universities (including
   public-private partnerships)?
